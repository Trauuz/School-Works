Campus Companion - Laboratory Exercise 3

Open CampusCompanion.xcodeproj in Xcode. Choose the CampusCompanion scheme
and an iPhone simulator, then press Command-R.

Implemented requirements
- CampusAnnouncement: immutable title, date, category, priority, and postedBy.
- Six sample campus announcements.
- Campus Announcements button below Explore Campus, linked by a Show segue.
- Storyboard UITableViewController with a custom AnnouncementCell prototype.
- Connected icon, title, and date outlets; Auto Layout and self-sizing rows.
- Row selection uses ShowAnnouncementDetailSegue and prepare(for:sender:).
- Existing personalized Explore Campus welcome flow is retained.
- Urgent announcements have a red warning icon, URGENT text, and tinted row.
- Detail includes all five announcement fields.
- configure(with:) explicitly resets styling for both urgent and normal rows.

Try it
1. Enter your name, select Faculty, turn notifications on, and add guests.
2. Tap Explore Campus and verify the personalized information, then go Back.
3. Tap Campus Announcements. Review all six rows and scroll the list.
4. Tap Campus Wi-Fi Maintenance Notice to see its Urgent priority and office.
5. Go Back and open a Normal announcement to compare.

Files to study
CampusAnnouncement.swift - data model and sample collection.
AnnouncementCell.swift - reusable row configuration.
AnnouncementsViewController.swift - data source, row selection, and data passing.
DetailViewController.swift - announcement or personalized welcome branch.
Base.lproj/Main.storyboard - prototype cell, outlets, constraints, and segues.

This is the completed custom-cell version (Parts 2 and 3), which supersedes
the temporary built-in Subtitle cells introduced in Part 1.
The earlier Downloads projects were not modified.
